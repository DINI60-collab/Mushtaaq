My letter

Take your time. It is long because you are worth the words.

My love,

I have been thinking a lot about us, about everything we have been through, and especially about the moments where I made you feel alone even though you had me. Loving someone is not only about having feelings for them in your heart. It is also about making that person feel loved, wanted, appreciated, heard and safe. There were times when I failed to give you that, and for that I am truly sorry.

I never wanted you to question your place in my life. I never wanted you sitting there wondering why I was not calling, why I was not giving you attention, or why I seemed distant. My actions sometimes made you feel like you were asking for too much, when you were only asking for the love you deserved from the person you love.

I am sorry for every moment you needed me and I was not there in the way you needed. I am sorry for every conversation where you wanted to feel close to me and felt distance instead. I am sorry for the times my silence made you overthink. You should never have had to wonder whether you were important to me.

The truth is that you are important to me in ways I struggle to explain. You are not just someone I love. You became part of my everyday life, my thoughts and my future. So many small things remind me of you — I see something and immediately think, I wish she was here so I could show her this.

I know life gets heavy. Some days my head is full of things I have to solve and I disconnect from everything around me. I do not want to use that as an excuse for hurting you. Being tired does not make your feelings less important. Being busy does not mean I should stop showing you that you matter.

And I understand something now that I should have understood earlier: you cannot see what is inside my heart. I can love you deeply, but if I do not call, do not talk to you, do not check on you, how are you supposed to feel it? I cannot expect you to read my mind. I have to show you.

That is what I want to change.

Not another beautiful paragraph full of promises, and then the same behaviour a week later. I want my actions to speak louder than my words. I want you to notice the difference without me having to point at it. I want to call you because hearing your voice makes me happy, not because I am afraid you will be upset. I want to listen when something is bothering you instead of defending myself.

I want to know what makes you feel loved. What makes you feel safe with me. The little things that make you smile and the ones that quietly hurt you. When you need me to talk, when you need me to listen, and when you just need to feel that someone is beside you.

I cannot erase what already happened. But I can take responsibility for it, learn from it, and make sure the lesson actually changes how I treat you. I do not expect you to trust every promise immediately. If I damaged something, rebuilding it takes time, and I would rather prove myself patiently than push you into believing words I have not earned yet.

I want us to build something better than what we had. New memories stronger than the painful ones. Laughing at stupid things again, staying up talking about everything and nothing, making plans, celebrating each other.

I do not want a relationship where two people say they love each other. I want one where two people actually feel loved.

When I have been distant, it was never because I stopped caring. Never because you became less beautiful to me or less worthy of my love. You are not what I am trying to escape. You are the person I want beside me while I figure my life out.

When I think about a better life one day, I do not only think about money or work. I think about having someone beside me to share it with. Someone who knows where I started, who remembers the hard days. That person is you.

So please accept my apology — for the pain I caused, for the moments I was absent, for every time my actions made you question my feelings. I love you. Not only when we are happy. Also through the difficult conversations, the misunderstandings, the quiet days.

I do not want to just tell you that you are my favourite person. I want to treat you like it.

My beautiful girl.
My favourite person.
My peace. My happiness. My future.
Forever yours, your husband 🐼❤️
